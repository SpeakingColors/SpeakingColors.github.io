Free music software that sounds good and stays free.

Synths, effects, DJ tools and controllers for your hardware synths. VST3 and standalone, built with JUCE and C++, with Windows and macOS builds for many.

How it works

Three steps.

1Pick something

Browse the synths, effects and tools. Everything is free.

2Download it

Grab the release zip from GitHub, or let the Gear Updater app do it for you.

3Open it in your DAW

VST3 plugins show up after a plugin rescan. Many also run standalone.

Free, open source, made by one person

Everything here is free to use. If it helps your music, a coffee keeps it coming.
