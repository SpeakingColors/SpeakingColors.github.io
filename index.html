How it works

Three steps.

01Pick something

Browse the synths, effects and tools. Everything is free.

02Download it

Grab the release zip from GitHub, or let the Gear Updater app do it for you.

03Open it in your DAW

VST3 plugins show up after a plugin rescan. Many also run standalone.
