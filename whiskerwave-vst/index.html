About Whiskerwave

Whiskerwave mixes wavetable oscillators that morph smoothly from one wave to the next with layered voices, a flexible modulation matrix and built-in effects. A sampling tool lets you turn your own hardware into new wavetables, and the included waves are free to use. It isn't released yet.

Status: test build. It installs and runs, but has not been checked on every machine yet.

What it does

  • Wavetable oscillators that morph between waves
  • Layered voices and a flexible modulation matrix
  • Built-in effects
  • Sample your own hardware into wavetables
  • Included waves are free to use for anything (CC0)
